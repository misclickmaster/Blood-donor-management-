Donor Eligibility Criteria

  • Age must be between 18 and 65 years.
  • Weight must be at least 45 kg (100 lbs).
  • Minimum 90 days gap between donations.
  • Healthy hemoglobin level (> 12.5 g/dL).

Health Benefits of Donating

  • Stimulates production of new red blood cells.
  • Balances harmful iron levels in the bloodstream.
  • Free mini-health checkup during pre-screening.
  • Psychological satisfaction of saving 3 lives.

Fast 3-Step Process

  • Register Online: Complete your donor profile with blood group and city.
  • Get Notified: Receive instant notification when an emergency matches.
  • Donate & Save: Visit the hospital or blood bank and save a life.
